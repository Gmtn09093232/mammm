Indexing Mechanism

1. INDEX CRANK
Rotate the handle by the calculated amount for each division.
The crank turns the worm shaft through the index plate at a fixed ratio (40:1).
→
2. CHANGE GEARS
Fit 48T driver and 32T driven gear for the required ratio (when using differential).
The change-gear train applies a small correction to the index plate rotation.
→
3. WORM DRIVE 40:1
Dividing head worm gear ratio 40:1 (standard).
The worm and worm wheel form the primary reduction stage.
→
4. WORKPIECE
Indexed workpiece ready for gear cutting.
The spindle is now positioned at the exact division angle for the next tooth.
ⓘ For non-standard ratios, use differential indexing with a change gear train.
